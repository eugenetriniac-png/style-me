4 · Mexico localization
74%of Mexican internet users buy clothing online — fashion is the top online category.
28%of online returns are fashion, the highest of any category (AMVO).
35%only rate their fashion purchase "very satisfactory": high interest, low conversion.
73%of consumers expected to buy second-hand — resale is mainstream, not niche.
65%worry about fast fashion's footprint (Ibero / Greenpeace) — but Shein still leads.

The contradiction is the finding: the same market that says it worries buys the most from Shein.
